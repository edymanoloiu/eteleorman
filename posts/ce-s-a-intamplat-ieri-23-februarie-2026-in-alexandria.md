On 22 February 2026, Alexandria witnessed a public rally organized by local environmental groups advocating for the preservation of green spaces amid urban expansion plans. The event gathered around 200 participants, including notable community leaders and activists, who called for a balanced development approach respecting nature and local quality of life. The city administration has acknowledged the concerns and plans to hold consultations in the coming weeks. _Sources: Adevărul, Teleuniversitatea_

## 🚨 Accidents

A traffic accident occurred late on 22 February at the intersection of Strada Mihai Viteazul with Bulevardul Alexandru Ioan Cuza, involving two cars and causing minor injuries to three individuals. Emergency services arrived promptly; the injured were taken to the county hospital for treatment and are expected to recover fully. Police investigations revealed that slippery road conditions due to early morning frost contributed to the collision. _Sources: Jurnal de Teleorman, Digi24_

## 💡 Economy

Alexandria’s local Chamber of Commerce announced a new initiative aimed at supporting small and medium enterprises (SMEs) by providing micro-grants and training programs starting March 2026. This initiative targets sectors hit hardest by recent market fluctuations, such as agriculture and small manufacturing, thereby fostering regional economic stability. Local business leaders have welcomed the plans, emphasizing their potential to stimulate job creation. _Sources: Ziarul Financiar, Teleorman Business Times_

## 🎓 Education

The city’s Technical College unveiled its updated curriculum focusing on green technologies and sustainable agriculture for the 2026 academic year. This development responds to growing demand from local industries seeking skilled workers versed in eco-friendly practices. The expansion includes workshops, internships, and partnerships with agricultural firms in the Teleorman region. School officials hope to attract more students and boost employment prospects locally. _Sources: Monitorul de Teleorman, EduNews Romania_

## 🏗 Urban Development

Alexandria Municipality presented a draft masterplan aimed at upgrading public infrastructure, including road repairs, new bike lanes, and renovations of several public squares. The document outlines a phased approach to begin work in late spring, with the goal of improving urban mobility and aesthetics. The plan invites public feedback before final approval, aiming for transparency and community involvement. _Sources: Alexandria City Hall official site, Romania Insider_

## 🌳 Environment

Recent environmental assessments confirmed that air quality in Alexandria remains within national safety limits, despite increasing traffic and industrial activity. Authorities continue monitoring pollutants and are planning additional green buffer zones near industrial areas to mitigate potential future risks. These measures align with national strategies for environmental protection promoted by the Ministry of Environment. _Sources: România Liberă, Agenția pentru Protecția Mediului Teleorman_

## 🏛 Local Politics

New appointments were made within Alexandria’s city council following last week’s vote, with Ioan Dumitrescu taking office as the new Deputy Mayor responsible for urban development and public works. The council also approved a budget amendment increasing funds for social programs targeting vulnerable groups. Political analysts view these moves as part of ongoing efforts to improve governance efficiency and address local priorities. _Sources: Hotnews.ro, Teleorman24_
